# Upload Product Designer Gym homepage v1 to GitHub

Repository: github.com/phoebeyudesign/ProductDesignerGym (served at phoebeyudesign.github.io/ProductDesignerGym/)

## What's in this folder
- `index.html`: the new homepage. It replaces the current `index.html` at the top level of the repository.
- `images/`: the logo and the two session photos. This is a new folder.

The `cafe/` and `design-system/` folders stay as they are. The new footer links to the design system, and the Build with AI card links to the cafe practice page.

## Steps (GitHub website, no tools needed)
1. Open the repository on github.com.
2. Click **Add file > Upload files**.
3. Drag in `index.html` **and** the whole `images` folder together.
4. Under "Commit changes", write: `Homepage v1: sessions, idea bank, house rules`.
5. Choose **Commit directly to the main branch** and click **Commit changes**.
6. Wait 1 to 2 minutes, then open phoebeyudesign.github.io/ProductDesignerGym/ to check.

## Updating later
Open `index.html` on GitHub, click the pencil icon, and edit the block marked `EDIT HERE` near the bottom:
- numbers in `stats`
- the next session in `nextSession`
- a new past session at the top of `pastSessions` (upload its photo to `images/` first)
- links in `links` (adding the LinkedIn link turns its "soon" button on)
