Code of conduct

House rules

A safe space to practise, learn and grow together.

  • Practise, don't perform

    Unfinished ideas and mistakes are the point.

  • Reps over results

    Progress comes from showing up and putting in the reps.

  • Give kind, specific feedback

    Talk about the work, not the person.

  • Respect everyone

    All levels and backgrounds are welcome.

  • Make room

    Share the time and let everyone have a go.

  • Speak up

    If something feels wrong, tell a facilitator and we'll sort it out.
